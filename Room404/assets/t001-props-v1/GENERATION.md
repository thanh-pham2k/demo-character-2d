The source requested a pair of textbooks, but generated a four-book stack; it is not a subject-specific book set. The ingredient tray and four bowls are each grouped props, not independently separated components.

Source `sheet.png` is 1774 × 887. Crop rectangles are recorded in `slice.py`; all visible alpha bounds remain within their cells, though the ingredients have a 7 px margin and the meal an 8 px margin. Each prop has a normalized PNG, native-resolution transparent PNG, quantized path SVG and `_tight.svg`; normalized canvas is 300 × 460 with bottom-center pivot, and tight SVG viewBoxes are derived from alpha bounds. Pixels per unit not applicable to web. SVGs are traces rather than hand-authored semantic layers. These props are draft assets and are not yet wired to the respective T-001 gameplay scenes.
