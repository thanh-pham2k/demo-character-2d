# Slipper removal and putting on

Built-in OpenAI image generation, one transparent 2 × 2 sheet, reference `unified-v1/plain_0.png`. Prompt: same boy crouching to remove green frog slippers; left column one slipper being removed, right column both feet in white socks and slippers beside/in hand; top row without backpack and bottom row with blue backpack. The reverse order is used for putting slippers back on.

`sheet.png` is 1254 × 1254; four 627 × 627 cells. Exact boxes and export names are in `slice.py`; no subject touches a cell edge. `plain_0`–`1` and `bag_0`–`1` are PNG and quantized SVG traces on a 300 × 460 canvas with bottom-center pivot and SVG viewBox 150 × 230. Pixels per unit: not applicable to web. SVG paths are raster traces, not independent editable body layers.
