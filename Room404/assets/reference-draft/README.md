# Reference extraction drafts

These assets are clipped from vector traces of the supplied room reference, not from the alternative sprite sheets. Their outlines are approximate; inspect edges and any baked-in occlusion before use. Some earlier drafts inherit the 768 × 512 trace's pixel-level detail; files ending `_original.svg` use the full-resolution reference. The original image remains in the main preview while the extraction is unfinished.

`ladder_left.svg` and `ladder_right.svg` preserve the visible pixels of each ladder from the supplied reference using a hand-drawn clip. The image hides part of the rungs behind the bed post; those hidden parts are not reconstructed. The right mask is mirrored from the left, and the edge masks are initial drafts, not a finished movable asset. `extract_ladder.py` records how it was created; run it from the repository root.

`*_original.svg` for the door, window, switch, bed posts, bedding, pillows, visible desk drawers, chairs, backpacks, lamps, laptops, book stacks, plants and tablet uses unquantized pixels from the original 1536 × 1024 reference, expressed as SVG paths and clipped to hand-drawn outlines. The right outlines are mirrored approximations, and all outlines are preliminary; the reconstructed desk surfaces replace areas hidden by props with a simple approximate gradient, the lower ends of the outer bed posts are covered by the shelves, the bedding still contains some bed rails and the pillows are partly covered by blankets, and the lower laptop edge and lamp bases are partly hidden by other objects in the source. Regenerate with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/extract_props.py` from the repository root.

`ladder_*_reconstructed.svg` adds hand-drawn approximate rungs and a rail where the reference is occluded. The original visible fragments remain as `ladder_left.svg` and `ladder_right.svg`; the reconstructed portions are inferred, not recovered from the source.

`laptop_*_reconstructed.svg` adds a simple inferred lower edge beneath the visible traced pixels. It can be regenerated with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/reconstruct_hidden.py`.

`bed_front_post_*_reconstructed.svg` extends the hidden feet with simple inferred vector shapes; the original cutouts remain available separately.

`desk_surface_*_reconstructed.svg` combines visible reference pixels with approximated areas and can be regenerated by `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/extract_desk_surface.py`.

`center_table_reconstructed.svg` combines the visible table pixels with approximate wood-color areas under the plant and tablet. Regenerate with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/extract_center_table.py`.

`rug_reconstructed.svg` keeps the visible rug pixels and approximates the section behind the table with vector stripes. Regenerate with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/extract_rug.py`.

`bookshelf_*_reconstructed.svg` retains visible reference pixels and approximates the surface beneath the plant; edges and obstructed portions are preliminary. Regenerate with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/extract_bookshelf.py`.

`placements.json` records normalized crop coordinates in the original image; it does not imply that the drafts are complete or that overlap order has been finalized.

`background_reconstructed.svg` places approximate wall and floor shapes behind the earlier traced background. Broad furniture regions are masked to expose an approximate wall and floor underlay. Edges, texture and lighting within those regions are approximations; the result has not yet been visually checked. Regenerate with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/build_background.py`.

The composite comparison uses the reconstructed variants of laptop edges, bed-post feet, ladders, desk surfaces, shelves, rug and center table.

`bed_left_composite.svg` and `bed_right_composite.svg` group the current bed layers into standalone SVGs with named groups; the drawn hidden parts and crop boundaries remain approximate. Regenerate with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/compose_beds.py`.

`desk_*_composite.svg` combines the extracted drawer parts with the reconstructed desktop in named SVG groups; the middle support and hidden desktop areas remain approximate or absent. Regenerate with `python3 Room404/assets/reference-draft/compose_desks.py`.

The extraction script discards pixels outside a one-pixel-expanded version of each outline before generating SVG paths. The SVG clip remains in place for the final edge; extraction outlines are still approximate.

The desk, shelf, rug and table generators also omit most source pixels that are concealed by their reconstruction masks; a small border remains to preserve mask-edge rendering.
